# Pricing

## Tiers

| Tier | Annual Price | Devices | Users | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Community** | Free | 3 | Unlimited | GitHub Issues |
| **Starter** | $9,000–$14,000 | 5 | 10 | Email (48h SLA) |
| **Professional** | $22,000–$35,000 | 25 | Unlimited | Email + Video (24h SLA) |
| **Enterprise** | $45,000–$85,000+ | Unlimited | Unlimited | Dedicated CSM + Phone |

## Add-Ons
- Onboarding & setup: $4,000–$12,000 one-time
- Custom Validation Package (IQ/OQ/PQ): $3,000–$8,000
- Custom Skill development: $5,000–$15,000 per Skill
- Annual penetration test report: $2,000–$5,000

## Why This Pricing Works
- 30–50% cheaper than Greenlight Guru / Smarteeva / Qualio
- Free tier captures startups + researchers + community contributors
- Validation Package add-on addresses the #1 adoption barrier in MedTech
- Enterprise tier includes dedicated CSM — matches what large companies expect

## Go-to-Market Strategy
1. Free pilots for first 10 customers (build case studies)
2. Launch Starter tier at $9k to undercut competition
3. Upsell to Professional as companies add devices
4. Enterprise tier with custom contracts for large manufacturers
