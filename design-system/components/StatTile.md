# StatTile

A bento KPI tile: kicker, a serif figure, a caption, and an optional cobalt sparkline.

**Provide:** an icon and label, the figure, a caption (deltas in `<b>`), and optionally a sparkline path.

- 3 columns × 2 rows. Figure in `figure-lg` (Newsreader 56, tabular). Caption `body-sm` `ink-2`; an upward risk delta in `critical`.
- Sparklines are single `accent` lines with a hollow end dot. No fills, no colour per tile.
