# AISummary

A Skill's narrative output on a cobalt tint, with footnote markers that resolve to verified sources.

**Provide:** one or two sentences with `sup.ms-fn` markers, the Skill name and version, its confidence, and the verified count; the sources as an `ol.ms-notes` list nearby.

- `accent-soft` tile, text in `deck` serif. The provenance line is `code` (Geist Mono) in `ink-3`.
- Every footnote must be a source that passed the citation verifier (openFDA, the CFR index, the guidance index). Confidence never reads above 0.95.
