# DeadlineRing

A countdown to a regulatory decision: a ring of days left, the rule, and the due date.

**Provide:** days left and the window length (ring fill = left / window), the rule and citation, the due date, and the start event.

- Ring track `surface-sunk`, fill `warning-mark`, figure in Newsreader 46. Kicker in `warning`.
- Use for 21 CFR 803 5-day and 30-day windows. Dates read "Nov 20, 2026".
