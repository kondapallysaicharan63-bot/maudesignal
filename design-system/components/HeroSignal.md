# HeroSignal

The ink tile that leads the overview: the most deteriorating signal, its headline figure and the next action.

**Provide:** a kicker (signal type, product code, severity), a headline naming the failure, a one-sentence deck ending with who decides, the headline figure and its caption, one primary and one secondary action.

- Spans 7 columns × 3 rows of the bento grid. The only `inverse` tile on a page.
- Headline in `headline` (Newsreader 44), deck in `deck` in `on-inverse-2`, figure in `figure-xl`. Kicker in `accent-on-inverse`.
- The concentric rings echo the logo's signal arcs at 16% `on-inverse`. Never add a photo or gradient.
